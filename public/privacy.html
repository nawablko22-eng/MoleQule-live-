Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026
This is a plain-language summary written for MoleQule Prep's students and parents, not a substitute for formal legal advice. If you need a legally-reviewed privacy policy for compliance purposes, have a lawyer adapt this before relying on it.

What we collect

When you sign up and use MoleQule Prep, we collect:

  • Your name, and whichever of phone number or email you sign up with (Google Sign-In accounts share the name and email Google gives us; phone sign-up accounts share the phone number you enter).
  • Course registrations, live class attendance, and video-watch activity, so we can show you your own progress and course library.
  • Order and payment records for paid courses — course, amount, coupon used (if any), and a payment reference from our payment provider. We do not see or store your card, UPI, or bank details; those are handled entirely by the payment gateway.
  • Basic usage events (page visits, course views) used only to understand what's popular and improve the course catalog.
  • A push-notification subscription, only if you turn on live-class notifications — you can turn this off any time.

How we use it

  • To run your account: log you in, show your registered courses, and give you access to videos and live classes you're entitled to.
  • To notify you when a live class is scheduled and again just before it starts, if you've enabled notifications.
  • To process payments for paid courses and keep a record of what you've purchased.
  • To apply referral and coupon rewards correctly.
  • To send a password-reset link if you ask for one.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data to anyone.
  • We don't share your data with third parties except the minimum needed to run the service — our payment gateway (to process a purchase) and our email provider (to send you a password-reset link), if those are set up.

Your choices

  • You can update your name, email, and phone number from your account at any time.
  • You can turn live-class push notifications on or off whenever you like.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data (registrations, cart, order history, coupons, referrals) permanently from your course page — this cannot be undone.

Questions

For any privacy question or request, contact YK Sir / MoleQule Prep through the usual channel you use to reach the course (Classplus, WhatsApp, or however you normally get in touch).
